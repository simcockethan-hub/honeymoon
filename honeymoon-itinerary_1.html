Saseka Tented Camp — 3 nights

In on the 24th, out on the 27th. Pack warm layers: August game drives are cold. The Greater Kruger is low-risk malaria, worth a word with your GP. A yellow fever certificate only applies if you've recently come from a yellow fever country, so it doesn't affect you.

Digital itinerary on safariportal.appReconfirmed 24 JulOutfit needed: safari casual

Pack neutral khaki, olive or brown — bright colors and white spook game — the green pants and long-sleeve shirt already on hand work well · warm layers for early and evening drives, temps drop fast · closed shoes, not sandals · a light scarf or buff for dust · binoculars if you have them, camp doesn't always supply them.

Admin

SARS online traveller declaration

New as of 1 July 2026: everyone entering and leaving South Africa files an online customs declaration within 24 hours of travel — so once on the way in, and again before you fly JNB → IST on 30 August. Keep the emailed confirmation on your phone. Nothing to declare still means you file.
